The little things add up

a lot to keep track of.

Lost papers, hard-to-find supplies, and a messy backpack can make school routines more stressful. Nested is being developed around a simple idea: give everyday belongings a place, so children can practice managing them.

THE NESTED CONCEPT

Meet your backpack's new organizing layer

Everything has
a place to land.

Nested is a backpack insert concept designed to create simple homes for the things kids carry every day.

✓

Spaces that make sense

A concept with designated spaces for papers, school supplies, snacks, and everyday essentials.

✓

Designed for real school days

An organizing layer intended to fit inside the backpack a child already uses.

✓

Practice that travels with them

Repeatable routines can help children learn where things belong and how to get ready on their own.
